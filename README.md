## Featured Projects

### ECONX - AI-Cloud National Statistics Platform
AI-powered cloud platform for the Statistics Department at iGA, Bahrain, automating the processing and analysis of financial statements and foreign investment data.
- **45-60 minutes to under 30 seconds** per financial statement (**99%+ faster processing**)
- Amazon Textract and Amazon Bedrock (Claude 4) for PDF extraction and validation
- AWS Cognito role-based access control, AWS Forecast predictions, and a Bedrock LLM chatbot for natural language data querying
- Officially approved, deployed, and launched for government use

`React` `TypeScript` `AWS CDK` `Lambda` `API Gateway` `Cognito` `S3` `CloudFront` `Aurora PostgreSQL` `Textract` `Bedrock` `Forecast` `Recharts` `ExcelJS`

### SHEDOO - Full-Stack E-Commerce & Business Management Platform
E-Commerce, CRM, Admin Management, Delivery, Customer Accounts, Inventory, Orders, Payments, and Business Operations in one ecosystem, with a centralized Admin Dashboard and financial/balance-sheet tracking.

`React` `TypeScript` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL` `Edge Functions`

### Government Hospital - Smart Nurse Shift & Workforce Management System
Rule-based scheduling engine replacing manual nurse scheduling, with leave and sick-leave requests, shift changes, approvals, attendance, overtime, and Excel/PDF schedule generation. Supports departments of up to 30 staff.

### DOO Asana Connector - Enterprise Integration & Automation Platform
Reusable integration layer connecting Asana with external systems through an API and MCP-based architecture. Expanded from **5 to 35 typed actions** with Zod validation, cursor-based pagination, client-side rate limiting, OAuth 2.0 with PKCE, and OpenAPI/connector specifications.

`MCP` `Zod` `OAuth 2.0 + PKCE` `OpenAPI`

### Brothers - Offline Sales & Business Management System
Arabic RTL desktop system for a carpet and furnishings business covering sales, invoicing, expenses, purchases, taxation, attendance, salaries, and employee management. Packaged as a standalone Windows application on a local SQLite database, working without an internet connection.

### TeleDerma - AI-Powered Dermatology Care Management Platform
Cross-platform Flutter mobile app connecting patients with dermatologists, clinics, and pharmacies across the GCC, with AI skin analysis, appointment scheduling, pharmacy integration, and an AI chatbot. Achieved an **84.6% usability score** using Nielsen's usability heuristics.

`Flutter` `Dart`
